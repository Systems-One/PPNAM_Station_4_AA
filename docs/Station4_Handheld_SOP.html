01

Before you start

Station 4 is the wastage station. This handheld does two jobs there, and they are separate on purpose: you register a collection wherever the bag is filled, and you weigh the bag later at the station's scale.

  • The scanner is charged and on the plant Wi-Fi.
  • You know your operator username and password, or you have your RFID badge.
  • For a weigh: the bag is physically on the Station 4 scale before you scan it.

Opening the app

The scanner boots into the PPNAM Station Launcher. Tap the purple Station 4 tile.

The app takes a few seconds to start and connect. Do not tap anything until the status pill in the top-right settles — see the next section.

02

The status pill

The coloured pill in the top-right corner is on every screen, and it is the single most important thing to be able to read. It tells you about two separate things that can go wrong: the broker (the network link) and Station 4 itself (the PC at the station).

Connected

Network is up and Station 4 is running. Everything works.

What to doCarry on.

Station offline

The network is fine, but Station 4's PC is not running or not reachable.

What to doCollections still work — they queue on the scanner and send themselves later. Weighs will not work: the scale is at the station. Tell a supervisor the station is down.

Reconnecting

The scanner lost the network link and is trying to get it back.

What to doWait a few seconds. If it does not clear, check Wi-Fi coverage where you are standing.

Offline

No network link at all. Nothing leaves the scanner.

What to doYou can still register collections — they queue safely. Move back into Wi-Fi coverage to let them send.

Why it lags a momentThe pill waits about 1.5 seconds before changing, so a brief network blip does not make it flicker. A pill that keeps swapping between Connected and Station offline means the station's PC is genuinely dropping in and out — report it.

03

Signing in

Everything you do is recorded against you, so the app will not do anything until you sign in. There are two ways.

By username and password

  1. Tap Username and type your operator name.
  2. Tap Password and type your password.
  3. Tap Log In. The button turns into a spinner while it works.

By badge

You do not have to tap anything first. While the Log In screen is showing, just scan your RFID badge — the app is already listening and will sign you in.

Fill in both boxesLeaving the password empty does not give you a clean “enter your password” message — it produces the confusing error “The server sent an unusable authentication challenge”. If you see that, check you actually typed a password.

If sign-in fails

The reason appears in red under the password box. The three you will actually meet:

  • No response received — Station 4 did not answer. Check the pill; if it says Station offline, the station is down. Wait for green and tap Log In again.
  • The server sent an unusable authentication challenge — almost always an empty password box. Type your password and retry.
  • Anything naming your credentials — wrong username or password. Retype carefully; the password box is masked.

Tapping Log In again is always safe. The app blocks a second sign-in from starting while one is already running, so a double badge-scan cannot sign in the wrong person.

04

The dashboard

Once you are in, you land on two tiles. That is the whole app.

  • Waste Collection (blue) — register a bag: what is in it, which job it came off, who filled it.
  • Weigh Bag (teal) — ask Station 4 to weigh a bag that is already registered.

The top bar

  • Your name and role sit top-left (Admin · Admin is name · role). Tapping it offers to log you out.
  • The gear opens Settings.
  • The pill shows the connection, as always.

A weigh is not a step of a collection. Register the bag first; weigh it whenever it reaches the scale.

05

Waste Collection

Five steps, then a review. The header always tells you where you are — Step 3 of 5 — Scan or enter the operator ID. On every step you can either pull the scanner trigger to read a barcode, or type into Manual entry and tap Submit. Scanning is faster and cannot mistype.

Backing outCancel transaction at the bottom of every step throws the whole bag away and returns you to Step 1. Nothing is sent. The back arrow at the top-left returns to the dashboard.

STEP 1Scan bag code

Scan the barcode on the wastage bag. This is the code that will identify the bag for the rest of its life, including when you come to weigh it.

STEP 2Scan or enter the job number

The job the waste came off. Scan the job card, or type it in.

STEP 3Scan or enter the operator ID

The operator who filled the bag. This is not necessarily you — you are recorded separately as the wastage operator who registered it.

STEP 4Select waste category

This step is a dropdown, not a scan. Tap the Waste Category box, pick from the list, then tap Confirm.

Short list?If the only choices are Uncategorised and one or two others, the scanner is still on its built-in starter list. Go to Settings → Refresh catalogue to pull the real list from Station 4. If you see “No waste categories available”, that refresh is not optional.

STEP 5Select waste type

A second dropdown, filtered to the category you just picked — for example WT-17 — Sweepings. Tap Confirm.

If this list is empty, you picked a category that has no types loaded. Refresh the catalogue in Settings.

REVIEWConfirm waste collection

Everything you entered, on one card, before anything is sent. Read it. Every row except the last has an Edit link that jumps you straight back to that step.

  • Bag code, Job number, Operator ID — what you scanned.
  • Waste category, Waste type — what you picked.
  • Wastage operator — you. This one has no Edit; it comes from your sign-in.

Cancel discards the bag entirely. Confirm sends it.

What happens after Confirm

The wizard immediately resets to Step 1, ready for the next bag, and a banner appears at the top:

  • “Queued WC-20260910-291041 for delivery” — the bag has a reference number. Write it down if you need to query the bag later.
  • “1 collection queued, awaiting delivery” — how many bags are still waiting to reach Station 4. This counts down on its own as they send.

Dismiss clears the banner. It does not cancel anything.

Collections are never lostA collection is stored on the scanner first and sent afterwards. If the pill is amber or red, keep working — the queue empties itself as soon as the link comes back, even if you close the app in between.

If a bag is rejectedA red banner reading “Bag <code> was rejected: <reason>” means Station 4 refused it after the fact. That bag did not register. Read the reason, fix it, and register the bag again. If the reason means nothing to you, take it to a supervisor with the bag code.

06

Weigh Bag

You are not weighing the bag — you are asking Station 4 to weigh it. Station 4 is the only machine wired to the scale. It measures, and records the result against your sign-in.

Nobody has to be at the station PCThe screen says so for a reason: do not go looking for someone to log in at Station 4 first. Your handheld session is the authority.

Do it in this order

  1. Put the bag on the Station 4 scale. Physically, first.
  2. Scan the bag's code, or type it into Bag code.
  3. Tap Request weight. It reads Weighing… while the station measures.
  4. Read the result, then take the bag off and scan the next one.

The bag must already be registeredA weigh is keyed only on the bag code. If you never ran a Waste Collection for this bag, Station 4 answers bag_code_unknown and refuses. Register it first.

A successful weigh

You get a card with the weight in large green figures, the bag code beneath it, who it was captured by, and the prompt “Scan the next bag.”

Weights show up to three decimal places with no padding — a clean 8.5 kg reads 8.5 kg, not 8.500 kg.

A refused weigh

An amber card tells you what went wrong in plain words. What matters is whether the bag code stays in the box:

  • The code is still there, with the line “The bag code is still here — fix the problem and tap Request weight again.” Fix the physical problem — steady the bag, put it on the pan, wait for the scale to free up — and tap again. Only three refusals behave this way: the scale is busy, the scale is empty, or the reading would not settle.
  • The box is cleared. That scan is finished. Do not keep tapping — do what the message tells you, then start again from a fresh scan.

A weigh is never queuedUnlike a collection, a weigh is not saved for later. It only means anything while the bag is on the pan. If it fails, it has to be done again there and then — there is nothing waiting in a queue to rescue it.

07

Settings

The gear icon, from any screen. The top half is open to everyone and is what support will ask you to read out. The bottom half needs a supervisor PIN.

Diagnostics — anyone

  • MQTT broker — the network link on its own, separate from the station. Green Connected here with an amber pill up top means the network is fine and the station is the problem.
  • Device ID — this scanner's identity, e.g. scanner_5c64df8d86a8. Read this out when you log a fault. It is fixed to the hardware and cannot be edited.
  • Version — the app build, e.g. v1.0 (1).
  • Catalogue — when the waste category and type lists last came from Station 4. “built-in seed — never synced” means the scanner has never pulled the real lists.
  • Refresh catalogue — tap this whenever the category or type dropdown looks wrong or empty. Safe to tap at any time.

Configuration — supervisors only

Type the supervisor PIN and tap Unlock. Five wrong attempts locks the box for 30 seconds; the app counts down your remaining attempts as you go.

Unlocking reveals the broker settings, and nothing else:

  • Host and Port — normally mqtt.sysone.co.za and 443.
  • WebSocket and TLS — both on for the standard deployment.
  • Username and Password — the broker account, not an operator account.

Test & Apply tries the new settings before saving them. On success it shows “Connected — settings saved” and re-locks itself after a couple of seconds. On failure it shows the connection error and saves nothing, so a bad setting cannot strand the scanner.

There is no topic to configureWhere messages go is derived from the device ID and is not editable, by design. If someone asks you to change a topic in Settings, there is nothing to change — the answer is the broker's access rules, not the scanner.

The rest of the panel

Scrolling down reveals the username and password fields and the Test & Apply button at the bottom. Changes do nothing until you tap it.

08

Finishing up

Handing the scanner over

Tap your name in the top-left and confirm Log out. You are asked first — “You'll need to log in again to continue” — because an accidental tap in the middle of a shift is annoying.

Always log out before passing the scanner to someone else. Whatever they register lands under your name otherwise.

Queued bags survive a logoutAnything still awaiting delivery stays queued and will still send. Logging out does not throw it away — but do not walk out of Wi-Fi range with a full queue if you can help it.

Closing the app

From the Log In screen, the hardware Back button asks “Close the app?” — tap Close to return to the launcher, or Stay to remain.

Back behaves in two stages here on purpose: if the keyboard is up, the first Back just closes the keyboard. Only from a settled screen does it offer to leave. You cannot drop out of the app by mistiming a tap.

Message reference

Every refusal Station 4 can give a weigh request, what it means on the floor, and whether your scanned bag code survives for a retry. Codes are shown because support will ask for them.

capture_not_confirmed The scale did not settle. Steady the bag and try again. The bag is swinging or leaning. Steady it, let it rest, tap again. Yes

collection_sync_conflict This collection is in conflict and a manager must review it. Set the bag aside and fetch a manager. Do not re-register it. No

CodeWhat you seeWhat it really means
schema_version_unsupported This app is out of date for Station 4. Ask a manager. The scanner needs an app update before it can be used.
device_id_mismatch This scanner's identity did not match. Ask a manager. A configuration fault. Quote the Device ID from Settings.

An unfamiliar messageIf the card names a code that is not in these tables — “Station 4 refused the weigh: <something>. Ask a manager.” — Station 4 is newer than the app on this scanner. Do not retry. Report the code and the bag code.

10

Known issues

Observed on this build (v1.0, build 1) during the walkthrough that produced these screenshots. Worth knowing so you do not chase a fault that is already understood.

  • An empty password gives the wrong error. Leaving the password box blank reports “The server sent an unusable authentication challenge” instead of asking for a password. Harmless, but misleading — check the password box before reporting a server fault.
  • Sign-in can time out with “No response received” even when the pill is green. If Station 4's PC is dropping in and out, the sign-in request can go out during a gap. Tapping Log In again while the pill is green normally works.
  • The catalogue ships empty-ish. A freshly installed scanner shows only the built-in starter categories until someone taps Refresh catalogue in Settings. Do this once when a scanner is issued.
